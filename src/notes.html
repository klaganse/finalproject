Understanding Notes

Notes tell musicians what pitch to play. In music, the main note names are A, B, C, D, E, F, and G.

Treble Clef Staff

From bottom to top the notes on the lines of the treble clef are E, G, B, D, and F.

How Notes Work

Sheet music uses a staff made of five lines and four spaces. Notes are placed on these lines and spaces to show pitch. The higher a note appears on the staff, the higher it sounds.

A helpful way to remember the treble clef line notes is: Every Good Boy Deserves Fudge. Make up your own acronyms too!

Line Notes

E, G, B, D, and F sit directly on the staff lines.

Space Notes

F, A, C, and E sit in the spaces between the staff lines.

Middle C

Middle C is often used as a starting reference point for beginners.
