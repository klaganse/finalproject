How It Works

This rhythm test creates a random one-measure pattern in 4/4 time. Press play and try clapping along with the clicks.

Quarter notes receive one beat, while eighth notes divide one beat into two equal parts. Half notes last for two beats, meaning they are held for twice as long as a quarter note.

Any note with a dot after it increases in length by half of its original value. For example, a dotted quarter note lasts for one and a half beats instead of only one beat.
