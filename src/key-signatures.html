Key Signatures

Key signatures tell musicians which notes are usually sharp or flat throughout a song.

What They Look Like

Key signatures appear at the beginning of a piece of music, right after the clef.

How Key Signatures Work

A key signature is a group of sharps or flats placed at the start of a staff. Instead of writing a sharp or flat every single time, the key signature tells the musician to automatically play certain notes sharp or flat.

For example, if the key signature has one sharp, it is usually F sharp. This means every F in the song should be played as F sharp unless the music says otherwise.

Sharps

Sharps raise a note by one half step.

Flats

Flats lower a note by one half step.

Natural

A natural cancels a sharp or flat.
