What is Sheet Music?

Sheet music visually represents music using notes and symbols.

About This Guide

Sheet music shows musicians what notes to play and how long to play them. It is made up of symbols like notes, rests, and rhythm.

This website will guide you through the basics, starting with notes, then moving on to rhythm and key signatures.
